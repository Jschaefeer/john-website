What to bring

Requirements depend on your coverage. Having these ready keeps check-in quick.

Medicare
Covered
Physician visits and X-ray services.
You pay
20% coinsurance, and the annual Medicare deductible when applicable.
HMO plans
Bring
Your insurance information, a copy of your insurance card, and your referral.
No referral
Payment is required at the visit. You'll be reimbursed once a valid referral is received, minus co-payment, coinsurance and deductible.
Co-payments
Collected at each visit.
Workers' Compensation and No‑Fault
Bring
All Workers' Compensation or No-Fault insurance information, plus attorney information, to your first visit.
Private insurance and self-pay
At your visit
If we don't participate with your plan, or you're uninsured, payment for the consultation, X-rays, supplies and any services is due on the day of your appointment.
Reimbursement
On request, we'll mail an itemized receipt you can submit to your insurance company.
Self-pay
Quality care should be accessible to everyone. We'll work with you to find an affordable solution.

Payment policy

As a courtesy, we submit claims to your insurance company on your behalf. You are responsible for the following.

Pay a bill online

Co-payments.

Due at the time of service for each visit.

Deductibles and coinsurance.

As determined by your insurance plan.

Referrals.

Required for HMO patients, and must be presented at the time of the visit.

Non-covered services.

Some services may not be covered by your plan. If your insurance deems a service not medically necessary, the balance is your responsibility.

Changes to coverage.

Let the office know right away about any change in your insurance or employment.

Past-due balances.

If a bill becomes past due, you are also responsible for any late fees or collection fees incurred.

Billing questions

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, money orders, personal checks, Visa, Mastercard and Discover. Co-payments and payment for services not covered by insurance are due when service is rendered. You can also pay a balance online.

Is there a fee for returned checks?

Yes. There is a $25.00 fee for all returned checks.

What does assignment of benefits mean?

By receiving services, you authorize Dr. John O'Hanlon to release any information acquired for purposes of treatment, payment and healthcare operations, and authorize payment to go directly to Dr. John O'Hanlon, D.P.M.

How is my health information protected?

As required by HIPAA, you will receive a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices. Your consent acknowledges that you understand how we use and protect your health information for treatment, payment and healthcare operations.

Questions about coverage?

The office is happy to help with insurance and billing questions.
