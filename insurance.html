Before your visit

What to bring

Requirements depend on your coverage. Having these ready keeps check-in quick.

Medicare

Medicare

Covered
Physician visits and X-ray services.
You pay
20% coinsurance and the annual Medicare deductible when applicable.
HMO

HMO plans

Bring
Your insurance information, a copy of your insurance card(s), and your referral.
No referral
Payment is required at the visit. You'll be reimbursed once a valid referral is received, minus co-payment, coinsurance and/or deductible.
Co-payments
Collected at each visit.
Workers' comp · No-fault

Workers' Compensation and No-Fault

Bring
All Workers' Compensation or No-Fault insurance information, plus attorney information, to your first visit.
Out of network

Private insurance and self-pay

At your visit
If we don't participate with your plan, or you're uninsured, payment for the consultation, X-rays, supplies and any services is due on the day of your appointment.
Reimbursement
On request, we'll mail an itemized receipt you can submit to your insurance company.
Self-pay
Quality care should be accessible to everyone. We'll work with you to find an affordable solution.
Practice policy

Payment policy

As a courtesy, we submit claims to your insurance company on your behalf. You are responsible for the following.

Co-payments
Due at the time of service for each visit.
Deductibles and coinsurance
As determined by your insurance plan.
Non-covered services
Some services may not be covered by your insurance plan.
Referrals (HMO)
Required for HMO patients and must be presented at the time of the visit.
Insurance changes
Notify our office right away of any change in insurance or employment status.
Medical necessity
If your insurance deems services not medically necessary or non-covered, the balance becomes your responsibility.
Past-due balances
If a bill becomes past due or delinquent, you are also responsible for any late fees and/or collection fees incurred.

Accepted payment methods

Co-payments and payment for services not covered by insurance are due when service is rendered.

  • Cash
  • Money order
  • Personal check
  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • Discover

There is a $25.00 fee for all returned checks.

Authorization and privacy

Release of information and assignment of benefits

By receiving services, you authorize Dr. John O'Hanlon to release any information acquired for purposes of treatment, payment and healthcare operations, and authorize payment(s) to go directly to Dr. John O'Hanlon, D.P.M.

HIPAA privacy practices

As required by HIPAA, you will receive a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices. Your consent acknowledges that you understand how we use and protect your health information for treatment, payment and healthcare operations.
