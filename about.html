Biography

Dr. O'Hanlon has been in private practice in Armonk and Somers since 1991, caring for patients of all ages throughout Westchester County.

A board-certified podiatrist and foot surgeon, he specializes in foot, ankle and leg conditions, with treatment plans tailored to each patient.

Combining advanced techniques with a compassionate approach, he focuses on both treatment and prevention, teaching patients how to keep their feet healthy through proper hygiene, footwear and routine check-ups.

Since 1991 he has been a staff surgeon at Northern Westchester Hospital, where he served as Chief of the Division of Podiatric Medicine and Surgery from 1997 to 2008.
